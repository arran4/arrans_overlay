Title: which_browser package rename
Author: Arran Ubels <gentoo@arran4.com>
Posted: 2026-08-14
Revision: 1
News-Item-Format: 2.0
Display-If-Installed: www-misc/which_browser

The www-misc/which_browser package has been renamed to www-misc/which_browser-bin
to properly reflect that it installs a pre-compiled binary package.

Users should update their systems and change their world file or package use flags
to point to www-misc/which_browser-bin instead of the old package.
