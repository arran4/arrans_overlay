Title: Jules retirement: clients and CLI removed

Google announced on 29 October 2026 that its Jules asynchronous coding
service will wind down on 31 December 2026. Users can finish Jules
sessions until 11 November 2026. After 11 November, Jules will be
read-only until the final shutdown.

Consequently, this overlay is retiring the following clients and their
automatic update workflows:

    app-misc/kjules
    app-misc/flutter-jules
    app-misc/flutter-jules-bin
    dev-util/jules-bin

Before shutdown, export any Jules settings, scheduled tasks, environment
variables, setup scripts, memories, and session history you wish to keep.
Code, branches and pull requests already stored on GitHub remain there.
Keep exports containing environment variables or other secrets secure.

If you installed these packages, remove them from the world set and
uninstall them when no longer needed:

    emerge --deselect app-misc/kjules app-misc/flutter-jules app-misc/flutter-jules-bin dev-util/jules-bin
    emerge --ask --depclean

Google mentions Antigravity as part of its ongoing agentic development
work, but has not announced automatic migration of Jules data to it.

Official announcement and export information:
https://jules.google/docs/changelog/2026-10-29
