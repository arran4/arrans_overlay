Title: Removal of dev-util/antigravity-bin and dev-util/antigravity-cli-bin in favor of GURU packages
Author: Arran <gentoo@arran4.com>
Posted: 2026-08-13
Revision: 1
News-Item-Format: 2.0
Display-If-Installed: dev-util/antigravity-bin
Display-If-Installed: dev-util/antigravity-cli-bin

The `dev-util/antigravity-bin` and `dev-util/antigravity-cli-bin` packages have been removed from this overlay because equivalent packages, `dev-util/google-antigravity`, `dev-util/google-antigravity-cli`, and `dev-util/google-antigravity-ide` are now available in the GURU repository.

Please migrate your installation to the GURU packages:

emerge --deselect dev-util/antigravity-bin dev-util/antigravity-cli-bin
emerge --ask dev-util/google-antigravity dev-util/google-antigravity-cli dev-util/google-antigravity-ide
emerge --depclean dev-util/antigravity-bin dev-util/antigravity-cli-bin
